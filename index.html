FAQ

Why does export take as long as the clip?

Clips are recorded in real time from the player, so a 30 second short takes about 30 seconds. Keep the page open and the screen on while exporting.

Why did it pick the same moments before?

Older versions ranked by raw loudness, which favors theme music and intros. Now speech activity and peaks relative to the scene around them are used, intro and credits are skipped, and picks are spread across the video. Tap “Different picks” for a new set.

Is the analysis AI?

It is signal analysis: speech activity, loudness peaks, motion and opening strength. It finds energetic moments, not meaning, so review the plan before exporting.

How does Smart reframe work?

Before a short is recorded, a small face detector (downloaded once) scans it for faces and scene cuts. Each shot then gets one steady 9:16 crop that follows the speaker only when they really move, and it jumps exactly on cuts. If the detector cannot load, it falls back to motion.

Can I load a YouTube link?

No. Browsers cannot download YouTube videos, and its terms do not allow it. Download your own video from YouTube Studio and choose the file. Direct .mp4 links work if the site allows it.

How do auto captions work?

A Whisper speech model runs inside your browser, only on the shorts you pick. The first run downloads the model. Urdu accuracy is much better with the Better or Best model. For large files, the short's audio is captured while the clip plays once, so it adds the clip length to prep time. In Export all, the next short's captions are prepared while the current one records.

What does Trim silence do?

While a short is recorded, ClipForge jumps over quiet stretches, so the result is tighter than the chosen length. Aggressive removes shorter pauses.

Which browser works best?

Latest Chrome or Edge (v126 or newer) on Android and desktop, or Safari. Shorts are always saved as MP4. Firefox cannot record MP4, so it is not supported.

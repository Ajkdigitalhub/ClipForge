FAQ

Why does export take as long as the clip?

Clips are recorded in real time from the player, so a 30 second short takes about 30 seconds. Keep the page open while exporting.

Is the analysis AI?

It is signal analysis: loudness, motion and opening strength. It finds energetic moments, not meaning, so review the plan before exporting.

How does Smart reframe work?

While a short is recorded, ClipForge watches movement across the frame and detects faces where the browser supports it, then glides the 9:16 window to the busiest area. It holds position when nothing changes.

Can I load a YouTube link?

No. Browsers cannot download YouTube videos, and its terms do not allow it. Download your own video from YouTube Studio and choose the file. Direct .mp4 links work if the site allows it.

How do auto captions work?

A small Whisper speech model runs inside your browser, only on the shorts you pick. The first run downloads the model and is slow on phones. Accuracy is lower for Urdu with the Fast model, so try Better and review the result.

What does Trim silence do?

While a short is recorded, ClipForge jumps over quiet stretches (no speech or sound), so the result is tighter and shorter than the chosen length. Aggressive removes shorter pauses. It needs the audio to be readable (files up to 300MB).

Which browser works best?

Chrome or Edge on Android and desktop. iPhone Safari has limited recording support.
